Backup, import and privacy

All records are stored in local browser storage. They do not automatically sync between phones or computers, and may be erased by browser cleanup. Export JSON backups often. GitHub Pages hosts the code, not your records.

Importing a backup replaces existing data. CSV files can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets. Do not store private financial identifiers in this publicly hosted app.
